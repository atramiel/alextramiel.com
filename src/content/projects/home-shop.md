I do electronics, wood, metal, home improvement and robotics work at home, so the shop has to switch jobs quickly.

## Benches

There are four workbenches: an adjustable-height one in the office for electronics, and three in the garage. The drawers are organized with Gridfinity baseplates so every tool has a spot and I can see what's missing.

I'm a DeWalt person for power tools, and I buy hand tools once: Wera, Knipex, Wiha.

## Printing

Two 3D printers do most of the fabrication, a Prusa Core One and a Bambu Lab machine. Over the years I've worked hands-on with FDM, SLA, SLS and PolyJet.

## Tracking filament

A self-hosted Docker stack on a Mac mini keeps track of filament and models. Spoolman holds the spool inventory, bridges report usage from both printers, and the print files live together on a shared network drive.

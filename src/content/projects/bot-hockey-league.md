The Bot Hockey League is competitive robot hockey: teams of remote-controlled robots, a puck, and goals. I help organize and run it as co-commissioner.

## Rules

A sport needs rules people can point to in an argument. I formalized the official BHL rulebook, now at version 1.1.

## Tournament format

For an eight-team, multi-day event I designed a format that starts with Swiss rounds and feeds into dual elimination brackets. Swiss rounds give every team a full set of games against evenly matched opponents, and the brackets settle who wins. We run the brackets on Challonge.

## Builders

Part of the job is helping other builders get their robots working. It's my favorite part.

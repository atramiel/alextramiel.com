I build combat robots across the full range of weight classes, from 150 gram machines that fit in a hand up to the 250 pound heavyweights. I've competed on BattleBots on television.

Combat is a harsh design review. Everything I've learned about specifying motors, picking fasteners and designing parts that survive abuse got tested in an arena first.
